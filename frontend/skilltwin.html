Market scorecard

Three explainable models fitted on the SAS CU hackathon datasets. Estimates based on that data, not the live job market. They run in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

1 · Junior skill profile → chance of a high salary hike

Scores on the 1–5 scale used in the JDS file. Logistic model, cross-validated AUC (small sample, so read it as indicative).
–

2 · Senior personality profile → chance of high success

Normalised Big-Five scores as in the SDS file. Quadratic discriminant model, which can express a zone; cross-validated AUC (small sample, so read it as indicative).
–

3 · Role and experience → market salary

Company-level average pay in the DataScience Jobs file (lakh per annum). Log-linear model, R² .
–
Decision support for self-development, not a hiring or pay decision tool. The models are fitted on small samples (139 and 161 records; 1,602 company rows), describe association and not cause, and may not transfer to other organisations. The two workforce files share a hidden row structure, so panels 1 and 2 are not independent evidence.
